Our Focus

What We Do for Every Learner

ArabLingo supports children with language and learning differences through early screening, awareness, and accessible educational support, with a focus on Arabic-speaking communities.

What Early Screening Does

Stronger Social Skills & Confidence

Early identification and support help children build friendships, communicate effectively, and feel included, reducing social isolation and promoting emotional well-being.

Improved Academic Performance

Children who receive early support are less likely to struggle with reading, spelling, or math, participate more actively in class, and achieve better learning outcomes.

Timely, Personalized Intervention

Screening allows for tailored therapy and educational plans, ensuring children receive the right support at the right time — setting them up for long-term success.
