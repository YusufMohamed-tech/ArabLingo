About Us

Empowering Communities Through Understanding

ArabLingo supports children with language and learning differences through early screening, awareness, and accessible educational support, with a focus on Arabic-speaking communities.

Our Mission

Driving systemic change to support cognitive and linguistic diversity in the Arab world.

Identify language challenges early, before they limit learning or confidence

Prevent educational exclusion by replacing misunderstanding with informed support

Equip families, educators, and schools with accessible tools and guidance

Advance better, child-centered approaches to language support and intervention
