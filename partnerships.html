Our Aspirations

Partnering with schools to ensure every child is understood.

ArabLingo aims to collaborate closely with the Egyptian Ministry of Education to integrate early language screening into elementary schools nationwide. By embedding these tools at the start of a child’s educational journey, we can:

Identify Early

Identify language and learning differences early, giving children the support they need before challenges become barriers.

Reduce Misdiagnosis

Reduce misdiagnosis and misunderstanding, ensuring interventions are accurate and timely.

Guide Children

Guide children toward personalized educational and therapy plans, tailored to their specific needs.

Proactive Approaches

Help schools adopt proactive, inclusive approaches, creating classrooms where every child can thrive.

Through this partnership, ArabLingo envisions a future where no child in Egypt is left behind simply because of language or learning differences.
